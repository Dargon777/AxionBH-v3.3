AxionBH · research workbench

AxionBH model explorer for rotating black-hole environments

A local interactive workbench for the stationary CVE closure, Bosenova, scalar superradiance and hybrid branches, with scenario comparison, parameter maps, sensitivity analysis, inverse diagnostics, accretion→plasma closure, gap and pair-cascade diagnostics, positron transport, and explicit model-validity layers.

Engine ready local-only · reproducible
Exploratory research calculator. AxionBH implements the current assumptions of the model; it is not a validated observational-astrophysics inference tool. In v8.9.1 the model is split into explicit layers. The stationary CVE closure is evaluated in natural units, while the a/M = 0.35 switch remains a phenomenological gate rather than a derived threshold.
